### 💡 Overview & Market Insights 🔍

This repository tracks top **commercial sales conversation intelligence SaaS platforms** and **open-source AI call analytics tools**. These solutions record, transcribe, and analyze B2B sales calls, customer success meetings, and contact center interactions to extract buyer intent signals, coach sales representatives, handle objections, and improve deal win rates.

#### 📈 Market Size & Structure Analysis
- **Estimated Market Size**: The global Sales Conversation Intelligence & Revenue Intelligence market is estimated at **\$2.8 Billion - \$3.5 Billion (2026)** and is projected to expand at a CAGR of **~18.5%**, reaching **\$6.5+ Billion by 2030**.
- **Market Dynamics**: The market is **moderately concentrated** at the top enterprise tier (dominated by platforms like Salesforce, Gong, Chorus by ZoomInfo, and Salesloft), while remaining **highly fragmented** in the mid-market, SMB, contact center QA, and self-hosted open-source developer spaces.

## ⚡ Open-Source GitHub Projects

Open-source sales conversation intelligence is accelerating rapidly. Below are top self-hosted repositories, speech engines, and AI frameworks sorted by GitHub Star Count (Descending).

### 🚀 Full Conversation Intelligence Platforms

- **[GTM Superintelligence (Attention)](https://github.com/attentiontech/gtm-superintelligence)** [<img stroke="none" fill="none" src="https://img.shields.io/github/stars/attentiontech/gtm-superintelligence?style=social&color=white" alt="Stars"/>](https://github.com/attentiontech/gtm-superintelligence/stargazers)  
  **Open-source GTM intelligence and sales call automation platform**.  
  - **Three-layer scoring**: Call (coaching), Deal (MEDDPICC/SPICED-grounded win-likelihood + slip risk), Account (CSM adoption, value, churn risk).
  - **30 post-call agents** spanning coaching, deal scoring, CRM sync, and account health.
  - **Claude Code skill + subagents + slash commands** — trigger coaching workflows directly from your terminal.
  - **Best for**: Revenue teams wanting flexible, agentic post-call intelligence.

- **[CXMind](https://github.com/Sonicwell/cxmind)** [<img stroke="none" fill="none" src="https://img.shields.io/github/stars/Sonicwell/cxmind?style=social&color=white" alt="Stars"/>](https://github.com/Sonicwell/cxmind/stargazers)  
  **Open-source real-time VoIP analytics platform with AI call monitoring** (BSL 1.1 / Apache 2.0).  
  - **Non-invasive HEP tapping** — deploy alongside existing PBX with zero telephony infrastructure changes.
  - **Real-time call analytics** — SIP/RTP packet processing supporting 300+ concurrent calls.
  - **AI agent-assist Chrome extension** for live agent coaching and PCI-DSS DTMF masking.
  - **Best for**: High-volume contact centers requiring real-time VoIP tapping.

- **[CallSense](https://github.com/aaron-seq/ML-voice-lead-analysis)** [<img stroke="none" fill="none" src="https://img.shields.io/github/stars/aaron-seq/ML-voice-lead-analysis?style=social&color=white" alt="Stars"/>](https://github.com/aaron-seq/ML-voice-lead-analysis/stargazers)  
  **Self-hosted alternative to commercial conversation intelligence tools** (MIT Licensed).  
  - **Explainable lead scoring** — 0-100 score showing the exact transcript quote behind every component.
  - **Objection detection & buyer intent** — flags 7 objection types and 10 BANT/MEDDIC intent signals.
  - **Deal risk flags & conversation metrics** — talk ratio, question frequency, and sentiment trajectories.
  - **Best for**: Transparent, evidence-based lead scoring and rep coaching.

### 🎧 Speech Transcription & Diarization Engines

- **[MOSS-Transcribe-Diarize](https://github.com/OpenMOSS-Team/MOSS-Transcribe-Diarize)** [<img stroke="none" fill="none" src="https://img.shields.io/github/stars/OpenMOSS-Team/MOSS-Transcribe-Diarize?style=social&color=white" alt="Stars"/>](https://github.com/OpenMOSS-Team/MOSS-Transcribe-Diarize/stargazers)  
  **Open-source speech transcription and diarization model** (Apache-2.0).  
  - **Long-form multi-speaker AI**: Single-pass inference on audio recordings up to 90 minutes long across 50+ languages.
  - **Won 1st place in INTERSPEECH 2026 MLC-SLM Challenge**.
  - **Best for**: State-of-the-art multi-speaker transcription and timestamping.

- **[Polyphon AI](https://github.com/polyphon-ai/polyphon)** [<img stroke="none" fill="none" src="https://img.shields.io/github/stars/polyphon-ai/polyphon?style=social&color=white" alt="Stars"/>](https://github.com/polyphon-ai/polyphon/stargazers)  
  **100% offline and private meeting transcription and speaker diarization engine**.  
  - **NeMo Sortformer neural diarization** with clean verbatim disfluency filtering.
  - **Local LLM integration** via llama.cpp/Ollama for summary generation with zero cloud data leakage.
  - **Best for**: Privacy-critical confidential sales and legal meeting analysis.

- **[Vezir](https://github.com/vezir-ai/vezir)** [<img stroke="none" fill="none" src="https://img.shields.io/github/stars/vezir-ai/vezir?style=social&color=white" alt="Stars"/>](https://github.com/vezir-ai/vezir/stargazers)  
  **Self-hosted team intelligence platform for meeting transcription & analysis**.  
  - **TEE-based hardware attestation** for confidential AI summarization presets.
  - **Cross-team sync without Git** using CLI artifact sharing.
  - **Best for**: Security-conscious teams needing hardware-enclave meeting privacy.

### 📊 Specialized Call Analytics & QA Tools

- **[OpDesk](https://github.com/Ibrahimgamal99/OpDesk)** [<img stroke="none" fill="none" src="https://img.shields.io/github/stars/Ibrahimgamal99/OpDesk?style=social&color=white" alt="Stars"/>](https://github.com/Ibrahimgamal99/OpDesk/stargazers)  
  **Real-time operator panel & call analytics for Asterisk PBX systems**.  
  - **12 KPI analytics cards**, period-over-period deltas, 7x24 heatmaps, and Silero VAD post-call talk ratio analysis.
  - **Best for**: Asterisk and FreePBX call center operators.

- **[Playcall](https://github.com/playcall-ai/playcall)** [<img stroke="none" fill="none" src="https://img.shields.io/github/stars/playcall-ai/playcall?style=social&color=white" alt="Stars"/>](https://github.com/playcall-ai/playcall/stargazers)  
  **Open-source Gong alternative for playbook-based call scoring** (MIT Licensed).  
  - **Scores calls against custom methodologies** (MEDDPICC, BANT, SPIN) and evaluates rep adherence.
  - **Best for**: Lightweight Vercel/Supabase call playbook scoring.

- **[OpenClaw Talk Analyzer](https://github.com/ZhenRobotics/openclaw-talk-analyzer)** [<img stroke="none" fill="none" src="https://img.shields.io/github/stars/ZhenRobotics/openclaw-talk-analyzer?style=social&color=white" alt="Stars"/>](https://github.com/ZhenRobotics/openclaw-talk-analyzer/stargazers)  
  **AI multi-source business conversation analyzer**.  
  - Extract tasks, speaker profiling, sentiment trajectories, and negotiation advice from call audio and chat logs.
  - **Best for**: Multi-channel customer conversation extraction.

- **[CallHarness SDK](https://github.com/callharness/callharness-sdk)** [<img stroke="none" fill="none" src="https://img.shields.io/github/stars/callharness/callharness-sdk?style=social&color=white" alt="Stars"/>](https://github.com/callharness/callharness-sdk/stargazers)  
  **Open-source analytics SDK for voice AI agents**.  
  - Evaluates STT/LLM/TTS latency, interruptions, tool calls, and post-call completion reasons with Pipecat integration.
  - **Best for**: Voice AI agent developers monitoring real-time agent calls.

- **[Nightcall](https://github.com/nightnoryu/nightcall)** [<img stroke="none" fill="none" src="https://img.shields.io/github/stars/nightnoryu/nightcall?style=social&color=white" alt="Stars"/>](https://github.com/nightnoryu/nightcall/stargazers)  
  **Automated phone call QA manager using OpenAI Whisper and Llama**.  
  - Automates call evaluation QA for phone sales teams to grade sales rep phone call compliance.
  - **Best for**: Automated phone sales QA auditing.

- **[django-twilio-call](https://github.com/django-twilio-call/django-twilio-call)** [<img stroke="none" fill="none" src="https://img.shields.io/github/stars/django-twilio-call/django-twilio-call?style=social&color=white" alt="Stars"/>](https://github.com/django-twilio-call/django-twilio-call/stargazers)  
  **Enterprise Django package for building Twilio call center apps**.  
  - Includes call recording, transcription hooks, real-time IVR, queue management, and WebSockets.
  - **Best for**: Developers building custom Python/Django Twilio telephony suites.
